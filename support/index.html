Your membership

Memberships are handled by Apple. To change or cancel yours, open the app’s Settings › Membership, or your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On a new phone, Settings › Membership › Restore purchases brings it back.

A new phone

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before, and your plan, workouts and food come back.

Reporting someone in your Circle

Press and hold anything shared to report it, or open the person’s page and choose Report or Block. We act on reports within 24 hours.

Deleting your account

In the app, Settings › Account › Delete account. It erases your account and its backup from our server and clears your phone. If you can no longer open the app, write to us from the email on your account.
