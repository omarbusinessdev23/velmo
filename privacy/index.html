Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Velmo runs your training and your eating on your iPhone and Apple Watch. What you log lives on your phone and in a private backup tied to your account. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and the app has no advertising or analytics trackers. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who we are

Velmo is made by Omar Naasan, Türkiye (“we”). We are responsible for the personal data described here. Write to us at velmosupport@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive an account identifier and, if you share them, your name and email address. We use them to keep your backup yours and to bring it back on a new phone.
  • What you tell us about you. Your first name, date of birth, height, goal and pace, from the opening questions or Settings. We use them to set your calorie and protein targets and your training plan.
  • What you log. Workouts, sets, cardio, the food you log, weigh-ins, body measurements and your settings. We use them to run your plan and show your progress, and back them up so you never lose them.
  • Apple Health, only if you allow it. Velmo can read your activity (steps, distance, energy, workouts), heart readings, sleep, and body measures such as weight. It uses them to set your targets, show how hard a session was and fill your Sleep card. It can write the workouts you record, their energy and heart rate, the food you log and your weigh-ins back to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared for marketing, and never stored in iCloud by Velmo.
  • The food reader, only after you agree. When you type a meal, say it, or photograph a nutrition label, Velmo asks once before anything is sent. The words or the label photo then go through our server to OpenAI or Google (Gemini), which return the foods and their amounts. Nothing else is sent with them: not your name, your body or your health data. We do not keep the photo after it is read. You can turn the food reader off in Settings.
  • Your voice stays on your phone. When you say what you ate, Apple’s speech recognition turns it into words on the phone itself. The recording never leaves it; only the words go to the food reader, as above.
  • How often you use the food reader. A count per day, kept so the reader’s daily limit holds.
  • Your Circle, if you use it. Your name, your face photo if you add one, and the people in your circle. Each day the people in your circle can see whether you trained, logged food and weighed in (a yes or no, never your weight or what you ate), your day’s steps and plan, your streaks, the cheers and nudges between you (with any words you add), and the cards you earned together. You choose which of these each person sees, in Settings. Photos you choose to send are seen by your inner circle only. Anything you share, such as a meal or a week of training, is removed after 60 days.
  • Inviting friends. When you invite someone from your contacts, Apple’s contact list opens on your phone and you send the text yourself. We never receive your address book.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report someone we keep the report (who, what, and your reason) so we can review it, and we act on it within 24 hours. Blocks are kept so the person you blocked cannot see or reach you.
  • Purchases. Memberships are bought through Apple. We receive a record that you are a member and when your membership renews, never your card details.
  • Crash reports. If you agreed to share with app developers, Apple may send us crash and energy reports. The app itself sends none.

Where it is kept, and who helps us

Your backup is stored by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). The companies that process data for us: Supabase (hosting and database), Apple (sign-in, purchases, Health on your device), Google (sign-in if you choose it, and Gemini for the food reader), and OpenAI (the food reader). OpenAI and Google may process food-reader requests outside the European Union; those transfers rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Our legal bases (EU and UK)

Contract, to run the app you signed up for. Your consent, for Apple Health, the food reader and your Circle; you can withdraw it in Settings at any time. Our legitimate interest, to keep the service secure.

If you live in Türkiye

Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) applies. We process your data on the bases above, with your explicit consent for health data and the food reader. Under its Article 11 you can learn whether and how your data is processed, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object, and complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu). Write to velmosupport@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Deleting your account erases your backup, your Circle profile and photos, the groups you own, your shares and your nudges from our server. Copies in our hosting provider’s encrypted backups age out within 35 days.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export and delete your data, restrict or object to how it is used, and complain to your data protection authority. Export and Delete account are in the app’s Settings; deleting an account that signed in with Apple also removes Velmo from your Apple ID’s sign-in list. For anything else, write to velmosupport@gmail.com.

Children

Velmo is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before any change that matters takes effect.
